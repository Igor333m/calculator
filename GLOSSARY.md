# Trucking Economics

This context describes business mileage and driver compensation used to assess trucking costs and earnings.

## Language

**Loaded miles**:
Business truck miles traveled while carrying a freight load.
_Avoid_: Miles driven, when referring only to loaded miles.

**Deadhead miles**:
Business truck miles traveled without a freight load, including empty-trailer, bobtail, and repositioning travel.

**Total business miles**:
All loaded miles and deadhead miles traveled for the trucking business.
_Avoid_: Total miles, when personal travel could also be included.

**Deadhead percentage**:
The share of total business miles that are deadhead miles.

**Deadhead running cost**:
The portion of fuel, DEF, and maintenance reserves attributable to deadhead miles. It is part of the total business cost budget.

**Gross freight revenue**:
The total payment earned for hauling freight, before fees and operating costs.

**Revenue per total business mile**:
Gross freight revenue divided by total business miles for the same trip or reporting period.
_Avoid_: Revenue per loaded mile, when referring to this measure.

**Owner draw**:
Money an owner withdraws from the business for personal use, tracked separately from business operating expenses.
_Avoid_: Driver salary, when referring to an owner's personal withdrawal.

**Maintenance reserve rate**:
The amount budgeted per total business mile for oil and service, tires, or repairs, including mileage traveled empty.

**Refundable escrow**:
Business funds held as security, separate from operating charges and freight revenue. Contributions tie up cash; releases make those funds available again.

**Net escrow contribution**:
Refundable escrow contributions less releases for the same reporting period.

**Cash available before owner draw and income taxes**:
Business revenue remaining after operating cash outflows, planned maintenance reserves, and net escrow contributions, before owner withdrawals and income taxes.
_Avoid_: Operating profit, net profit, when the amount includes truck payments and planned reserves.

**Cash remaining after owner draw**:
Cash available after the planned owner draw, before income taxes; a negative amount is a funding shortfall.

**Cash available per total business mile**:
Cash available before owner draw and income taxes divided by total business miles for the same reporting period.

**Business break-even rate**:
The gross revenue per total business mile required to cover operating cash costs, revenue-based fees, and planned maintenance reserves, before escrow movements and owner draw.

**Owner-draw target rate**:
The gross revenue per total business mile required to cover the business cost budget, net escrow contributions, and the planned owner draw for the reporting period.

**Company-driver gross pay**:
Compensation a company driver earns before taxes and withholding, distinct from the truck's freight revenue and operating costs.
_Avoid_: Truck profit.

**Effective company-driver pay per mile**:
Company-driver gross pay per total business mile driven, whether the truck is loaded or empty.
